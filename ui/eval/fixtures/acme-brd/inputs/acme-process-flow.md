# Acme Equipment Hire - Process Flow

## Reservation

A Reservation represents confirmed future demand. Equipment is held against the Reservation until delivery.

## Quote

A Quote is an initial commercial proposal. A Quote can convert to a Reservation or directly to an Agreement.

## Billing

The billing method is selected when the Agreement is created. Supported billing methods are Weekly, Monthly and 28 Day billing. Invoices are generated on the schedule the billing method sets until the Agreement closes.

## Delivery

Delivery cannot be planned until an Agreement exists. Delivery follows this sequence: Agreement, Allocation, Checkout, Delivery Planning, Delivery, Confirmation. Delivery may capture signatures and photos.

## Returns

On return the branch records the return date and meter readings. Final billing may include rental charges and cleaning charges.
